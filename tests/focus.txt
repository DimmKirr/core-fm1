# FM Lab -- a tap and a hold are two different buttons.
#
#   build/host/fmlab build/host/focus tests/focus.txt
#
# EDIT and ENV answer to how long you hold them. Held, they open their page, as they always did.
# Tapped, they stay on HOME and swing the four knobs round onto one operator -- level, ratio and
# detune for EDIT, attack, decay, sustain and release for ENV.
#
# The hard part to test is that a tap really did nothing but repoint the knobs. So the knobs are
# driven to known numbers while focused, and then the page is opened -- the long way, deliberately
# -- and the bars read there. A bar is the parameter's amount in pixels, so the claims come out as
# arithmetic rather than "the screen changed".
#
# `btn 6 600` holds EDIT for 600 ms, which is past the threshold. `btn 6` on its own is a tap.
0 btn 8
100 shot

# A tap of EDIT. The diagram is still there -- this is HOME -- but the knobs and the legend are not.
300 btn 6
500 shot

# KNOB1 is now OP1's level, and nothing else's. Drive it to a number we can check for: 50, which
# is ten clicks, because a click of a 0..99 parameter is worth five.
700 enc 2 -99
800 enc 2 10

# The sixth black key is OP6. While focused it points at an operator instead of sounding, so this
# must send no MIDI at all -- and must move the knobs onto OP6.
1000 keys 0x2000
1100 keys 0

# A white key, though, is still a key: F3 has to play.
1300 keys 0x1
1500 keys 0

# OP6's level to the floor, which is a different number from OP1's and proves they are separate.
1700 enc 2 -99
1900 shot

# Now hold EDIT. That is the page, on the operator the black key picked.
2100 btn 6 600
3000 shot

# The first black key points back at OP1, whose level we left at 50.
3200 keys 0x2
3300 keys 0
3500 shot

# A tap of ENV from the page comes back to HOME with the envelope under the knobs instead.
3700 btn 4
3900 shot

# KNOB1 is ATTACK. Eight clicks is 40 of it, which is rate 1 of 59 underneath.
4100 enc 2 -99
4200 enc 2 8

# Held, ENV opens on the ADSR bank: the attack cell has to read 40.
4400 btn 4 600
5300 shot

# and again walks on to the raw bank, where rate 1 has to read 59 -- the same number, backwards.
5500 btn 4 600
6400 shot

# HOME, a tap of EDIT, and HOME again: the cross-operator knobs have to come back.
6600 btn 8
6800 btn 6
7000 shot
7200 btn 8
7400 shot
7800 end
