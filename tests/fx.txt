# FM Lab -- the filter on FX: a tap puts it under the knobs on HOME, a hold gives its page.
#
#   build/host/fmlab build/host/fx tests/fx.txt
#
# One filter on the finished sound, LOW PASS, BAND PASS or HIGH PASS, or off. The sound is checked
# by measuring the harmonics of one steady note -- ORGAN on middle C has partials at 131, 262, 523,
# 1046 and 1570 Hz and holds them -- once with the filter off and then through each setting, so
# what the filter took away is the difference and the voice cancels out.
#
# The screens: a tap of FX on HOME puts CUTOFF RESONANCE TYPE under the knobs and the filter's
# curve over the spectrum, from the diagram or the spectrum alike; a second tap steps the type; the black keys go on playing, because none of it belongs to an operator; and
# a hold of FX gives the FILTER page, the curve over three cells.
200 enc 6 10
400 shot

# ---- a tap: the filter's knobs on HOME, and the spectrum with its curve -- flat, because it starts off
600 btn 2
1000 shot

# the note everything is measured against, through no filter at all
1200 keys 0x80
2400 keys 0

# LOW PASS at 230 Hz: the fundamental through, everything above it going
2600 enc 2 -99
2700 enc 2 7
2900 btn 2
3200 shot
3400 keys 0x80
4600 keys 0

# two more taps, BAND PASS and then HIGH PASS, at 1.86 kHz: the other way round
4800 btn 2
4900 btn 2
5000 enc 2 6
5200 shot
5400 keys 0x80
6600 keys 0

# round to LOW PASS again, and with SEL held for single steps, at 1.06 kHz -- on the eighth harmonic
6800 btn 2
7000 btn 2
7100 btn 3 800
7200 enc 2 -8
7500 shot
7600 keys 0x80
8800 keys 0

# the same, with all the resonance there is: the harmonic under the cutoff should stand up
9000 enc 3 20
9200 shot
9400 keys 0x80
10600 keys 0

# the black key the panel prints OP1 under is still a key here: nothing on the filter is an
# operator's
10800 keys 0x2
11400 keys 0

# HOME leaves the filter's knobs, and the diagram comes back
11600 btn 8
11800 shot

# ---- the spectrum, and a tap of FX over it: the curve on the sound
12000 btn 8
12200 btn 2
12400 keys 0x80
13000 shot
13200 keys 0
13400 btn 8
13600 keys 0x80
14200 shot
14400 keys 0

# ---- a hold: the FILTER page, the curve over three cells
14600 btn 2 600
15400 shot
15600 enc 4 1
15800 shot

# the button whose page this is walks its banks, and it has only the one
16000 btn 2
16200 shot
16400 end
