# One filter on the finished sound, LOW PASS, BAND PASS or HIGH PASS, or off. It lives on HOME's
# spectrum screen: there, with nothing else on the knobs, they are CUTOFF RESONANCE TYPE SPAN, and
# its curve is drawn over the sound. The sound is checked by measuring the harmonics of one steady
# note -- ORGAN on middle C has partials at 131, 262, 523, 1046 and 1570 Hz and holds them -- once
# with the filter off and then through each setting, so what the filter took away is the
# difference and the voice cancels out.
#
# FX is then the effects: a tap puts the four effects' MIXes on HOME's knobs, a second tap and SEL
# leave them there, HOME takes them off, and a hold gives the EFFECTS page, its own button walking
# the four effects and the next hold opening where the last one left off. What they do to the sound is effects.txt's business.
200 enc 6 10
400 shot

# ---- HOME once: the spectrum, the filter's knobs, and its curve -- flat, because it starts off
600 btn 8
1000 shot
