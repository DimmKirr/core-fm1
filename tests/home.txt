# FM Lab -- the four knobs on HOME, and whether they really are the operators they claim to be.
#
#   build/host/fmlab build/host/home tests/home.txt
#
# HOME has no parameter rows, so there is nothing on the screen to read a knob off. The proof has
# to come from the other end: turn a knob here, walk to the EDIT page, and look at the bar that
# draws the byte. If the bar moved by the right amount, the knob was wired to the byte.

# It boots on the diagram with the first set of knobs: LVL 1 to 4.
200 shot

# SELECT steps the set. Four of them, and then it comes back.
400 enc 0 1
600 shot
800 enc 0 1
1000 shot
1200 enc 0 1
1400 shot
1600 enc 0 1
1800 shot

# The analyser is where the filter lives, so its four knobs are the filter's: CUT RESO TYPE SPAN.
2000 btn 8
2200 shot

# Back to the diagram, then out to OP1's own page to see where its level starts.
2400 btn 8
2600 btn 6 600
3400 keys 0x02
3500 keys 0
3700 shot

# KNOB1 on HOME is OP1's level: six clicks down, which is thirty steps of the byte -- a click of
# a 0..99 parameter is worth five -- and so 69 pixels of bar.
3900 btn 8
4100 enc 2 -6
4300 shot
4500 btn 6 600
5400 shot

# The third set puts ratios under the same knobs. Five steps up the list, and the second bar -- the
# one that draws the ratio -- has to be the one that moved.
5600 btn 8
5800 enc 0 1
6000 enc 0 1
6200 enc 2 5
6400 btn 6 600
7300 shot
7700 end
