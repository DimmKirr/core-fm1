# The claim the drawing makes is that you can watch an envelope move. So: pick an algorithm with a
# deep stack and a pair beside it, hold a note, and take the picture several times on the way down.
# A modulator and a carrier with different envelopes have to come apart, or the picture is lying.

# A plucked voice, so there is a decay to watch rather than a flat hold. The preset comes first:
# loading one brings its own algorithm with it, and would undo the choice below.
0 enc 6 12

# Algorithm 1: 6>5>4>3 on one side, 2>1 on the other, feedback on OP6.
100 enc 1 -31

300 keys 0x80
450 shot
900 shot
1700 shot

# Let go, and let it die away properly: the boxes have to empty, not freeze where they were.
2400 keys 0
3400 shot

# The selected operator switched off: struck through, and contributing nothing.
3600 btn 10
3800 keys 0x80
4000 shot
4200 keys 0
4400 btn 10

# Algorithm 32 is six carriers side by side -- the tightest the boxes ever pack, and the case where
# one box's live redraw could reach into the next one.
4600 enc 1 31
4800 keys 0x80
5000 shot
5200 keys 0
5600 end
