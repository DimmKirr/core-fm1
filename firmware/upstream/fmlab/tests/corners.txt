# FM Lab -- the two corners: the battery, and which of HOME's screens you are on.
#
#   build/host/fmlab build/host/corners tests/corners.txt
#
# Sixteen pixels across the top that nothing else wanted. The battery is three bars because three
# is all the precision there is: the divider and the ADC reference are both undocumented, so the
# thresholds are measurements, and this script drives the raw counts across every one of them.
#
# The pips are the other end. They are a HOME thing -- the other pages already say 1/4 in their
# titles -- and the case worth checking is the one a signature cache gets wrong: a page change
# clears the whole screen, so the corners have to come back by themselves.

# A full battery, on HOME's first screen.
200 shot

# HOME again: the second pip, and nothing else up there moves; and again, the third.
400 btn 8
600 shot
650 btn 8
750 shot

# Off to a page. The pips go, the battery stays, and both of those are after a full-screen clear.
800 btn 6 600
1700 shot

# Down through every threshold. 591 and up is three bars, 561 two, 531 one, and under that the
# outline turns red with nothing left in it.
1900 batt 570
2000 shot
2200 batt 540
2300 shot
2500 batt 500
2600 shot

# And back to HOME, where the pips have to return.
2800 batt 800
3000 btn 8
3200 shot
3500 end
