# FM Lab -- operator waves: the eight shapes WAVE gives an operator, heard, drawn and saved.
#
#   build/host/fmlab build/host/waves tests/waves.txt
#
# INIT VOICE is one operator, OP1, on its own as the carrier: whatever shape it is given is what
# comes out, so each wave is played on middle C and its harmonics measured -- SINE has none, SQUARE
# only the odd ones, SAW all of them, HALF SINE a strong second, ABS SINE nothing at the note itself.
#
# The screens: a tap of EDIT draws the selected operator's wave in its box, and LEVEL shrinks it; on
# HOME, a box that is not a sine wears a small mark of its shape. And the wave is saved with the
# voice: leave the slot, come back, and it is still a saw.

# the first user slot, INIT VOICE
200 enc 6 20
400 shot

# ---- a tap of EDIT: WAVE is the fourth knob, and OP1's box draws a sine
600 btn 6
900 shot
1000 keys 0x80
2200 keys 0

# HALF SINE
2400 enc 5 1
2600 shot
2700 keys 0x80
3900 keys 0

# ABS SINE
4100 enc 5 1
4300 shot
4400 keys 0x80
5600 keys 0

# SQUARE, three further on
5800 enc 5 4
6000 shot
6100 keys 0x80
7300 keys 0

# SAW
7500 enc 5 1
7700 shot
7800 keys 0x80
9000 keys 0

# LEVEL down shrinks the drawing, and back up
9200 enc 2 -60
9400 shot
9500 enc 2 60

# WAVE is a ring: one past SAW is SINE, and one back from SINE is SAW
9550 enc 5 1
9600 shot
9620 enc 5 -1
9650 shot

# ---- plain HOME: the bar, and the saw's mark on it
9700 btn 8
10000 shot

# ---- save it where it is, leave the slot and come back: the mark and the sound are still a saw
10200 btn 9
10300 btn 9
10600 enc 6 -1
10800 enc 6 1
11000 shot
11200 keys 0x80
12400 keys 0
12600 end
