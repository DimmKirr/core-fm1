# FM Lab -- the operator boxes on HOME: five bars and, while you are editing one, a curve.
#
#   build/host/fmlab build/host/levels tests/levels.txt
#
# Most of the time a box is a bar that fills from the left with how loud that operator is at this
# instant, and the claim that makes is about time: a plucked voice has to empty its boxes as it
# decays, and letting go has to take all of them with it.
#
# The operator the knobs are on is the exception. Tap ENV and that one box draws its envelope
# instead -- solid, so a box that small still reads, ending in the bottom right-hand corner, and
# without its number, which the white ring round it has already made unnecessary. That one is
# worth a shape because it is the one you are looking at; the rest stay bars, which is what makes
# it stand out.

# A plucked voice, so there is a decay to watch rather than a flat hold. The preset comes first:
# loading one brings its own algorithm with it, and would undo the choice below.
0 enc 6 12

# Algorithm 1: 6>5>4>3 on one side, 2>1 on the other, feedback on OP6.
100 enc 1 -31

# Nothing playing: six outlines with their numbers in them, and nothing filled in anywhere.
400 shot

# Now hold it, and take the picture on the way down.
700 keys 0x80
820 shot
1400 shot
2400 shot

# Let go, and let it die away properly: the boxes have to empty, not freeze where they were.
3000 keys 0
3400 shot

# The selected operator switched off, under EDIT: struck through, and contributing nothing.
3650 btn 6
3700 btn 3
3900 keys 0x80
4100 shot
4300 keys 0
4500 btn 3
4600 btn 8

# Algorithm 32 is six carriers side by side -- the tightest the boxes ever pack, and the case where
# one box's live redraw could reach into the next one.
4700 enc 1 31
4900 keys 0x80
5100 shot
5300 keys 0

# And the one box that is not a bar. Back to algorithm 1, tap ENV, and look at a screen with
# nothing sounding on it at all: one box has to have grown a curve. Then a tap of EDIT for LEVEL
# under KNOB1, turn it down, and back to ENV: the curve has to be visibly lower. A bar would have
# been empty in both.
5600 enc 1 -31
5900 btn 4
6200 shot
6300 btn 6
6400 enc 2 -70
6500 btn 4
6700 shot
7000 end
