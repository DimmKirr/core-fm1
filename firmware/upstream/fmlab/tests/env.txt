# FM Lab -- the envelope said twice: ADSR on the first two banks, the four raw segments after them.
#
#   build/host/fmlab build/host/env tests/env.txt
#
# ADSR is not a new thing stored anywhere. It is the same eight bytes the DX7 has always had, with
# three of them turned round so that a bigger number means a longer time, and two of the knobs
# writing a second byte so that what they promise is what you get. Everything here is a claim about
# those bytes, and the raw banks are where it goes to collect the evidence.

# ENVELOPE, bank 1/5: all four of ADSR at once, a cell to a knob. Drive each to an end first so
# the run does not depend on what the preset happened to hold. A click of a 0..99 parameter is
# worth five, so the counts below are a fifth of the values they land on.
200 btn 4 600
1000 enc 2 99
1100 enc 2 -8
1200 enc 3 -99
1300 enc 3 5
1400 enc 4 -99
1500 enc 4 14
1600 enc 5 -99
1700 enc 5 9
1800 shot

# 2/5: rate 1 and level 1. Rate 1 has to be 99 minus the attack.
1900 btn 4 600
2700 shot

# 3/5: rate 2 and level 2.
2800 btn 4 600
3600 shot

# 4/5: rate 3 and level 3 -- both of which DECAY and SUSTAIN had to have written as well.
3700 btn 4 600
4500 shot

# 5/5: rate 4 and level 4. Rate 4 has to be 99 minus the release.
4600 btn 4 600
5400 shot

# And round to the start again, unchanged by the walk.
5500 btn 4 600
6300 shot

# A tap of ENV while already on ENVELOPE is the next bank, the same as another hold. It used to
# drop to HOME with the knobs focused instead, which is the one thing the button cannot sensibly
# mean from here -- you are on its page, so asking for focus would be asking to leave it.
6600 btn 4
7400 shot

# the same bank reached the long way, to compare against
7600 btn 8
8000 btn 4 600
8900 btn 4 600
9800 shot
10000 end
